To Agape,

Dear Agape,

I have often wondered what it must be like to be loved by someone like you.

Someone so gentle with the world, so genuine with the people in it, so full of a kindness that seems to come naturally. Someone who gives so much of herself to the people she loves, who carries the weight of being a good daughter, a good sister, a good friend, and still finds room in her heart to dream of becoming more.

I wonder if you know how rare you are.

If you know that there is something beautiful about a person who makes goodness seem effortless. Someone whose heart makes friendship feel safe, whose honesty makes trust feel easy, and whose existence makes another person want to be better simply because they have had the privilege of knowing her.

And perhaps that is what frightens me most about you. How easy it seems to imagine falling in love with you. Not because I have mistaken admiration for love, but because, if love is to be built upon friendship, gentleness, honesty, and the quiet decision to keep choosing someone, then you seem like the kind of person one could spend a lifetime learning to love.

I hope God gives you everything your heart has been brave enough to dream of. I hope you become everything you are meant to be, and that life is as gentle with you as you have been with others. And I hope that, on the days when the world is unkind, you have someone beside you to remind you that you deserve the very same tenderness you give so freely.

Because tears are expensive things, Agape. They cost pieces of a heart that has already given so much. And although I cannot promise you a life without sadness, I would hope to be someone who never makes your heart feel that it must endure its sadness alone.

Perhaps I am getting ahead of myself. After all, what could a secret admirer possibly know about spending a lifetime with someone they have never met?

Perhaps all of this is hypothetical. Perhaps I have simply imagined a girl so wonderful that the thought of knowing her, holding her hand, watching her dreams unfold, and growing alongside her seems almost too good to be true.

Or perhaps, somewhere along the way, admiration became a wish. A wish to be the person you tell your stories to at the end of the day. The person who gets to witness your victories, sit with you through your difficult seasons, pray with you over your dreams, and choose you even when choosing you requires effort.

A wish to call you mine.

And perhaps, if this were a different kind of letter, I would tell you that I have met you before. That there was an art gallery, and a day that quietly changed the way I imagined my future. That I have questioned many things, but never quite managed to convince myself that my heart was wrong about you.

With love,
Your not-so-secret admirer,
Remmy
